# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code and outcomes, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish logs containing personal data without consent.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for behavior they deem inappropriate or harmful.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
